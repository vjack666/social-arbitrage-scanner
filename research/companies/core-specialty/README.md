## Daily update — 2026-11-19

### Newly extracted filing data / reported figures
- **Confirmed:** the Form S-1 was publicly filed on 2026-11-16. As of the latest materials reviewed, the company has not disclosed a final IPO price range or share count; the proposed listing is under ticker **CORE** on the NYSE, subject to the listing process.
- **Reported by Reuters from the filing (2026-11-16):** for the nine months ended 2026-09-30, total revenue was **$2.16B**, versus $2.55B a year earlier; net income applicable to shareholders was **$68.8M**, versus $111.8M; net premiums written were **$2.23B**, versus $2.61B; and the combined ratio improved to **93.0% from 98.2%**. These figures show improved underwriting ratio alongside lower reported revenue and earnings.
- The filing and coverage indicate existing shareholders are expected to sell shares; the company is not currently describing a primary capital raise. Do not assume proceeds to Core Specialty.
