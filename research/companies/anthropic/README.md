## Social-arbitrage hypotheses

1. Strong public attention may create demand exceeding fundamentals; measure attention separately from verified customer and financial evidence.
2. Developer adoption and enterprise contracts may be leading indicators, but GitHub stars, social mentions, or product enthusiasm are not revenue.
3. The first trading sessions may be dominated by float, allocations, index expectations, and momentum rather than long-term fundamentals.
