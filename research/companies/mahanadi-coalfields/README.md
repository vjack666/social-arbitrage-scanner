## Daily update — 2026-11-19

### Officially marketed IPO; day-one demand developing
- **Reported offer terms corroborated across financial coverage:** Mahanadi Coalfields' IPO opened on **2026-11-19** and is scheduled to close **2026-11-23**. The price band is **₹129–₹136**, with approximately **661.8 million shares** offered by promoter Coal India, a 100% offer for sale. Mahanadi Coalfields itself receives no proceeds. The reported issue size is about **₹89.81 billion** (₹8,981 crore), with listing tentatively expected 2026-11-27.
- **Anchor allocation reported 2026-11-18:** about **₹22.95 billion** was allocated to 53 funds at ₹136 per share. LIC was reported as the largest anchor participant. This is a disclosed allocation reported from exchange filings, not evidence of guaranteed aftermarket gains.
- **Day-one subscription (reported, 2026-11-19):** market-data coverage reported approximately **0.38x overall subscription**, with qualified institutional buyers at **1.26x**, while retail and non-institutional categories remained below 1x. Subscription figures change during the day; preserve timestamp when comparing later snapshots.
- Earlier reports of a roughly 5% grey-market premium are unofficial sentiment indicators and are not exchange prices or reliable forecasts.

### Updated interpretation
The QIB book is covered in the reported snapshot, but total subscription is still below 1x and retail/NII demand is weak at that snapshot. This is mixed demand evidence, not a final verdict; check end-of-day and final-day subscription and compare offer valuation against earnings, production growth, coal prices and policy risks. As a 100% OFS, the event is principally a promoter divestment rather than growth capital for Mahanadi.
